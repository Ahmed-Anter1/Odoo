# Product Brand Management in Inventory

## Overview

A reusable Odoo product-brand model integrated with product and inventory workflows.

## Implemented Capabilities

- Dedicated product-brand records
- Brand assignment on product templates
- Brand-based product counts
- Brand visibility in inventory quantities
- Search and filtering by brand
- Brand menus and management views
- Data consistency through relational fields

## Technical Areas

Odoo 18, Product, Inventory, Python models, computed fields, search views, list/form views, menus, and access controls.

## Engineering Focus

Brands are modeled as structured business records rather than free text, enabling consistent filtering, reporting, and future brand-level automation.
